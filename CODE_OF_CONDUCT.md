# Code of conduct

CodeArena exists so students can practise, compete and learn together. Anyone who takes part in this project (the
code, the issues, the discussions, the live site) is expected to help keep it a respectful place.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give feedback that helps: say what you saw and what you would change.
- Keep contests fair: solve problems yourself, do not share solutions while a contest runs, and do not attack the
  judge or other participants (see the [contest rules](apps/web/app/rules/page.tsx) and
  [terms](apps/web/app/terms/page.tsx)).
- Respect other people's privacy and time. Do not publish anyone's personal data or private code.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, sexual or violent content, doxxing, spam, and deliberately breaking
the service or someone else's work.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions that break this code, and may limit or remove accounts on
the live site. To report a problem, write to **soumiryasarangi@gmail.com**; reports are handled privately.

This text is short on purpose. It follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
